Zijun Wang

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at University of California, Santa Cruz (UCSC), advised by Prof. Cihang Xie in the VLAA Lab. I received my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University.

My research focuses on autonomous agents, data-centric LLM training, and AI safety and alignment.

I am currently interning at Amazon in Palo Alto, working on RSI agents and research world models with Hanqing Lu.

I am always open to research discussions and collaborations : )
